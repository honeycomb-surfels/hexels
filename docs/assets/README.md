# Figures and logo

- `hexels-logo.svg` and `hexels-logo.gif`: artwork and bee-flight/honeycomb animation from the [project website](https://honeycomb-surfels.github.io/), copyright 2026 honeycomb-surfels, under its MIT license. The GIF preserves the animation on Markdown hosts.
- `hexel-method.webp`: the method figure from the [paper and project website](https://honeycomb-surfels.github.io/#teaser), copied without modification.
- `hexel-cells.png`: existing research visualizations of DTU scan65 (skull) and scan83 (toy). The first training camera is shared across each row. The panels show Hexel rendering, visible-cell boundaries and base-plus-spatial color. They retain active cells with opacity at least 0.2 and use the dataset foreground mask and the same crop. They illustrate independent overlapping cells, rather than a fused or watertight reconstruction. Dataset imagery retains the terms listed in the [dataset guide](../datasets_2dgs.md).
